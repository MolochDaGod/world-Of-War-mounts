---
name: Toon RTS alliance factions
description: The player-facing faction structure for Race Wars.
---

Race Wars has exactly three playable faction identities:

- Human Alliance: Humans + Barbarians
- Elf-Dwarf Alliance: Elves + Dwarves
- Orc-Undead Warhost: Orcs + Undead

**Why:** The allied races fight together under one banner; presenting the six races as independent factions makes the faction picker and army-builder fiction incorrect.

**How to apply:** Only show the three alliance identities in player-facing faction selectors. Allied regiments must retain their own race metadata so they continue to use their correct character meshes inside a mixed alliance roster.
